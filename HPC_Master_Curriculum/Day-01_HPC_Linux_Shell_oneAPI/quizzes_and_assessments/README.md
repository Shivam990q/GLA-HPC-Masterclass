## 🔒 Examination Compliance Specifications
- **Sheet 1 Format:** Strict 7 columns conforming to university evaluation portals:
  `Sl no` | `Questions` | `Option a` | `Option b` | `Option c` | `Option d` | `Answer ( only mention option a,b,c,d)`
- **Answer Values:** Single lowercase letters (`a`, `b`, `c`, `d`).
- **Sheet 2 Content:** Complete Instructor Evaluation Key with pedagogical rationales and mathematical derivations.
- **CSV Synchronization:** Bit-for-bit synchronized with Sheet 1 for automated portal ingest.
