5. AVX-512 Vectorization: Density & Contiguous Memory

Say this: “Scalar instructions compute 1 number per cycle. AVX-512 registers are 512 bits wide: packing 8 double-precision (float64) or 16 single-precision (float32) numbers into a single clock cycle! FMA (Fused Multiply-Add) computes (A × B) + C in 1 clock!”
